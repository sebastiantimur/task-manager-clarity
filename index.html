    Help & frequently asked questions

    Keep a small list of clear, practical tasks. Start with something you can finish today.

    How do I add a task?

    Type a description, then choose Add Task or press Enter. Use a specific action, such as “Review lecture notes”. Blank descriptions are not accepted.

    How do I complete a task?

    Select the checkbox beside the task. Completed tasks are crossed out. Select it again to reopen the task.

    Can I hide completed tasks?

    Uncheck Show completed tasks to focus on unfinished work. Check it again to bring completed tasks back into view.

    How do I delete a task?

    Choose Delete beside the task you no longer need. Deletion is permanent, so check the task description first.

    Will my tasks be saved?

    Tasks are stored in this browser when local storage is available. They do not sync between browsers or devices. Clearing browser data can remove them.

    What should I avoid entering?

    Use fictional tasks for this usability activity. Do not enter passwords, personal information, or sensitive details.
